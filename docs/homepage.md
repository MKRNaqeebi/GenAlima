## GenAlima Home page

### Part 1: Homepage Copy

#### **Hero Section**

* **Headline:** **Ask Questions. Get Answers. From All Your Business Data.**
* **Sub-headline:** Stop hunting through Google Drive, emails, and documents. Our AI agent connects to all your data sources, finding the exact information you need in seconds.
* **Primary CTA Button:** `Start Your 14-Day Free Trial`
* **Secondary Text below CTA:** *No credit card required.*

#### **Social Proof Section**

* **Text:** **Trusted by innovative small and medium businesses worldwide**
* **(Placeholder for Logos):** [Logo 1: InnovateCorp] [Logo 2: MarketBoost] [Logo 3: Solutions Inc.] [Logo 4: Growth Partners]

#### **Problem/Pain Point Section**

* **Headline:** **Stop Searching. Start Knowing.**
* **Body:** Your company's most valuable asset is its knowledge, but it's trapped. Scattered across different apps and inboxes, finding one piece of information can feel impossible.
* **Pain Points (with checkmarks or 'X' icons):**
  * Wasting hours every week hunting for files and conversations.
  * Making critical decisions based on incomplete or outdated information.
  * Onboarding new team members takes forever because knowledge is siloed.

#### **Solution / How It Works Section**

* **Headline:** **Your Data, Unified and Intelligent, in 3 Simple Steps**
* **Step 1:**
  * **Title:** **Connect Your Sources**
  * **Description:** Securely link your apps like Google Drive, Slack, Notion, Outlook, and more in just a few clicks. Our system is built with enterprise-grade security.
* **Step 2:**
  * **Title:** **Ask Anything**
  * **Description:** Use simple, natural language to ask questions. No complex queries needed. For example: "What were our Q2 sales figures for the 'Alpha' project?"
* **Step 3:**
  * **Title:** **Get Instant Answers**
  * **Description:** Our AI agent instantly synthesizes information from all connected sources to give you a direct answer, complete with links to the original documents.

#### **Features as Benefits Section**

* **Headline:** **More Than Search. It's Your Business Superpower.**
* **Benefit 1 (Icon: Clock):**
  * **Title:** **Reclaim Your Time**
  * **Description:** Slash the time your team spends searching for information by up to 80%. Focus on high-value work, not digital scavenger hunts.
* **Benefit 2 (Icon: Chart/Graph):**
  * **Title:** **Make Smarter Decisions**
  * **Description:** Get a complete picture by drawing insights from every corner of your business. Uncover trends, track project progress, and act on data, not guesswork.
* **Benefit 3 (Icon: People/Team):**
  * **Title:** **Unify Your Team's Knowledge**
  * **Description:** Create a single source of truth for your entire organization. Ensure everyone is working with the most current, accurate information, instantly.

#### **Testimonial Section**

* **Headline:** **Why Business Owners Choose Us**
* **(Placeholder Testimonial):**
  * **Quote:** "This tool is a game-changer. I used to spend hours digging through emails and Drive for client info before meetings. Now, I just ask a question and get a full summary in 30 seconds. We're more prepared and our clients have noticed."
  * **Attribution:** **Jane Doe, CEO of a Marketing Agency**

#### **Security & Trust Section**

* **Headline:** **Your Data is Yours. Period.**
* **Body:** We understand your data is sensitive. Security isn't an afterthought; it's our foundation.
* **Trust Points (with lock icons):**
  * **End-to-End Encryption:** Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
  * **Read-Only Access:** We never modify your original files.
  * **Strict Privacy:** Your data is never, ever used to train third-party AI models.

#### **Final Call-to-Action (CTA) Section**

* **Headline:** **Ready to Unlock the Power of Your Data?**
* **Body:** Stop letting valuable knowledge hide in plain sight. Find what you need, when you need it, and empower your team to do their best work.
* **Primary CTA Button:** `Start Your 14-Day Free Trial`
* **Secondary Text below CTA:** *Free for 14 days. Cancel anytime.*
